HANDA is a disaster incident reporting and household assessment module for the eGovPH Super App, built for the eGovPH Hackathon 2026. It takes real-time incident intake through eReport, analyzes citizen check-in needs, deduplicates households, tracks non-respondents, and exposes public APIs for third-party integration.
