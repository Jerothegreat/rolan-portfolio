Champion at UMak's INFOTECH'26 Hackathon as team Blub Blub Blub. We built HeronsGuide, a campus hub that gives each UMak college its own announcements, reminders, and a chatbot that answers only from the official documents. Every answer cites its source section, and anything outside the sources gets "Information not in provided sources" instead of a guess.

Department admins publish dated announcements and upload or replace source files from an admin dashboard, and a failed replacement never removes the active source. Students reach the chatbot inside UMak's TBL Hub as an embedded widget or on Telegram. Retrieval is scoped to each college, and a grounding check turns any uncited answer into the fallback.
