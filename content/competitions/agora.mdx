Mayari is a real-time bilingual (Taglish) AI voice agent for Philippine medical clinics. Patients talk to it through Agora Conversational AI, which runs the live speech-to-LLM-to-speech loop. It collects symptoms for intake, assesses urgency, and books the right specialty's slot, while deterministic safety rules override the model for high-risk symptom patterns.

After the hackathon, the voice pipeline moved from Agora to LiveKit.
